About Us

Welcome to the Bristol Regional Food Network Digital Marketplace, a platform designed to connect local food producers directly with customers in the community.

Our mission is to support sustainable agriculture, reduce food miles, and strengthen local economies by making fresh, locally sourced food more accessible.

Our Vision

We believe that communities thrive when people have access to fresh, locally produced food. By creating a direct connection between producers and customers, we aim to promote transparency, sustainability, and healthier food choices.

What We Offer:

A marketplace for locally produced food.

Direct purchasing from trusted producers.

Seasonal and sustainable food options.

Support for small farms and businesses.

Convenient ordering and collection options.
